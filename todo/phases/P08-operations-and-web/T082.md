# T082 — Run shadow and soak validation

> Phase: `P08`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T072, T073, T080, T081

## Outcome

paper pipeline is stable over an agreed observation window.

The window runs on an authorized paper configuration: `G-PAPER-AUTHORIZE-01` requires the
active-`PoolKey` selection, both tokens' `HOLD`/`LP` approvals and the preliminary-paper
authorization to be completed by the Owner through T073's Web entry point before any paper
run. A window that begins before T073 is `APPROVED` and those three records exist, or that
continues after one of them is superseded or no longer bound to the active pool and
strategy version, is not the observation the SLOs and the final report may claim.

## Deliverables

predeclared duration/pools/SLOs, daily completeness/reconciliation,
incident log, resource profile, restart/provider-failover drills, final report.

The window record names the three T073 authorizations it ran under, so the evidence
identifies the authorized configuration and not only the duration and the pools.

## Acceptance

zero unexplained gaps/ledger drift/safety bypass; every SLO breach has
disposition and rerun evidence; duration is chosen before results are viewed; the window
starts only after T073 is `APPROVED` and the three authorization records exist for the
active pool and strategy version, and a window that began without them, or that ran while
one of them was superseded or mismatched, is reported as invalid evidence naming the
affected interval rather than counted as SLO evidence (test).

## Must not

reset evidence to hide an incident, shorten the window after failure, start or continue
the window before the three T073 authorizations exist, or count a window with a superseded
or mismatched authorization as SLO evidence.
