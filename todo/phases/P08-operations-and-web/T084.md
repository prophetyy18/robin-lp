# T084 — Build the authenticated read-model Web console

> Phase: `P08`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T025, T072, T080, T109

## Console scope in P08

`ARCHITECTURE.md` §2.2 assigns `robinhood_lp.web` to the P08 web-console tasks
`T084`–`T086` and records two further P08 web-module rows outside that
triple: the strategy-registry and run surfaces owned by `T087` (`WEB-PAGE-012`, which
lists registered strategies and triggers, monitors and cancels a run) and the backtest
result surfaces owned by `T088` (`WEB-PAGE-013`, the layered backtest result page). Those
two pages are owned by `T087` and `T088`; they are explicitly outside this task's
acceptance and this task neither requires nor blocks them, so the P08 console page set is
complete only once they are delivered too. `T083` owns the preliminary-paper release
dossier (`robinhood_lp.ops.dossier`) and carries no `WEB-*` requirement.

The dataset version, the member pools with their own block ranges and the reporting
numeraire that `WEB-GLOBAL-001` and the dataset part of the `WEB_CONSOLE.md` section 4
provenance bullet require are displayed from the manifests and reports this console
reads, which `T109` delivers and this task already depends on. The displayed manifest
provenance includes the registered strategy identity and the parameter-schema and registry
revision the manifest records, plus its canonical T100 dataset content hash and
partition-reference provenance and simulation-evidence schema/checksum/availability, rendered
as recorded rather than invented, substituted or rewritten. This shell displays those bindings;
it does not reconstruct `MarketState`, project `RunState`, compose `ReplayFrame`, or duplicate
the T104 fee projection owned by the dedicated T088/T103 pages. The registry-wide question — which datasets exist, what each
covers and its qualification status — belongs to the dataset registry
`T100` delivers in P10, so the requirement is scoped to what P08 can observe: where no
dataset stands behind a surface, the console shows `WEB-GLOBAL-002`'s explicit
unknown/absent state rather than a value, a carried-forward version or a fabricated one.
`T100` is not a dependency of this task and this task neither requires nor blocks it.

## Outcome

the owner can inspect discovery, research, strategy, paper and health evidence
without developer tools or access to secrets.

## Deliverables

authenticated local Web shell and read APIs; global chain/mode/token/
PoolKey/strategy/dataset version and reporting numeraire/freshness/pause banner;
candidate-pool dossier; backtest comparison; paper/live-shaped position, Range,
liquidity, inventory, USDG valuation, PnL attribution, intent and health views;
explicit empty/partial/conflicting/stale states; and manifest/evidence provenance showing the
registered strategy identity, schema/registry revision, canonical dataset reference and
simulation-evidence availability the `T109` artifacts record, with a pre-T109 `T105` or `T063`
artifact rendered as explicitly legacy and non-current.

## Acceptance

every `WEB-GLOBAL-*`, read-only `WEB-PAGE-*` and visualization requirement
owned by the P08 web-console tasks `T084`–`T086` maps to component/API tests; the four
pages owned by T103 — `WEB-PAGE-008` data coverage and downloads, `WEB-PAGE-009` replay
and position inspector, `WEB-PAGE-010` dataset builder and `WEB-PAGE-011` model
laboratory — lie outside this task's acceptance and are neither required nor blocked by
it; the two P08 pages owned by `T087` and `T088` — `WEB-PAGE-012` and `WEB-PAGE-013` —
also lie outside this task's acceptance and are neither required nor blocked by it; the
dataset version, member block ranges and reporting numeraire the console shows
are the ones the manifests and reports it reads record, and a surface with no dataset
behind it shows the explicit unknown/absent state rather than a value; the provenance
shown for a manifest includes the registered strategy identity, schema/registry revision,
canonical dataset reference/content hash, evidence schema/checksum and replay availability it
records, and no display path substitutes a free-text, inferred or legacy value for one (test);
current, pre-T109 and T063 provenance render distinguishably, each legacy or
evidence-unavailable artifact carrying an explicit non-current/unavailable marker and never
appearing as current provenance (test); a manifest whose recorded dataset, registry/schema
revision or evidence checksum disagrees with the current `T109` artifact fails closed and shows
the mismatch rather than a value (test); inspection proves this shell neither stores a duplicate
market/run projection nor treats evidence as approval, execution or promotion authority, and no
rendered provenance contains a secret (test); paper/testnet/live cannot be confused; raw IDs/provenance remain
inspectable; browser restart does not stop background operation.

## Must not

fetch private material, mutate domain state from a read model, hide failed
evidence, substitute a free-text, inferred or legacy identity, dataset, revision or evidence
binding for the one a manifest records, present a pre-T109 `T105` or `T063` artifact as current
provenance, duplicate a market/run/fee projection, infer authority from replay evidence, or treat Web
availability as strategy availability.
