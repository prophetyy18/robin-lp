# T083 — Produce the preliminary-paper release dossier

> Phase: `P08`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T082, T107

## Outcome

a reviewer can decide release readiness from evidence, not claims.

## Deliverables

versioned install/runbook, architecture/threat model, supported-pool
matrix, dataset/experiment/soak manifests, test/security scan results, known limits,
rollback and explicit statement that this evidence alone does not authorize live.

The experiment and candidate evidence is drawn only from the current registry-bound T107
candidate lock: the dossier names the released candidate version, the registered strategy
identity, the registry/schema revision that produced it, and the lock's content hash and
actor. A T066 lock stays an immutable historical record — byte-identical, never rewritten
or backfilled with a registry identity, and never promoted, cited as the released
candidate or counted as current release evidence — and evidence that would need a missing,
mismatched, downgraded or unavailable registry/schema revision blocks the affected release
claim instead of falling back to it.

## Acceptance

clean-room reproduction succeeds; every checkbox links to evidence;
unresolved critical/high issues block release and medium issues have owner/date; the
released candidate resolves to its current registry-bound T107 lock, whose recorded
identity, registry/schema revision and content hash the dossier reports and re-reads
(test); an old-format T066 lock, a lock whose content hash or registry/schema revision
disagrees, and an unavailable registry revision are refused as release evidence and block
the affected release claim rather than being promoted (test).

## Must not

call the release production/live ready or omit losing backtests/incidents; or promote a
legacy T066 lock, an absent lock, or a mismatched or downgraded lock into current release
evidence.

## References

T107 (the registry-bound candidate lock and experiment evidence this dossier resolves its
released candidate to), T082 (the shadow/soak evidence it packages),
`docs/intent/PROJECT_GOALS.md` (`G-STRATEGY-REGISTRY-01`, the registry-bound identity rule
this dossier's candidate evidence must satisfy).
