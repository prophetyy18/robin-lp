# T110 — Qualify exact historical market and run-state replay

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `OWNER_BOOTSTRAP_SPLIT_2026_09_21`

## Dependencies

T040, T041, T052, T061, T100, T104, T109

## Outcome

The product can read canonical historical market state and the exact saved position/accounting
state of one successful T109 run at the same cursor, and compose them into a replay frame without
rerunning strategy code, duplicating the market timeline or creating another accounting engine.

## Deliverables

Define `MarketState(dataset_version, pool_key_id, cursor)` as post-event state resolved from T100's
immutable partitions and T040/T041 reconstruction. A block-only cursor means end-of-block.
Persisted reconstruction checkpoints are sparse checksummed projections bound to dataset hash and
reconstruction revision, never another source of market truth. Pre-first-event reads return the
recorded initial state.

MarketState excludes fee-growth fields T040/T041 do not reconstruct. Readers requiring global,
outside or inside fee growth or exact range fees compose T104's cursor-addressable projection with
the same dataset hash, window and reconstruction revision and retain that provenance beside the
value. T110 must not replace, fork or reimplement T104.

Define `RunState(run_id, cursor)` as a projection from T109's immutable simulation evidence through
the existing T061 engine/accounting semantics without any strategy callback. It begins from the
recorded initial state, selects the latest valid sparse checkpoint, and applies every later
transition at or before the requested cursor in cursor/ordinal order. A checkpoint identifies both
its cursor and last applied ordinal. End-of-block includes every transition in the block.

Define `ReplayFrame(run_id, cursor)` as read-time composition of dataset-bound MarketState and
same-cursor RunState. Validate run, dataset hash/version, PoolKey, range, cursor, engine/accounting/
reconstruction revisions and all checksums before returning it. Missing, mismatched, failed,
cancelled, incomplete or pre-evidence inputs fail closed with named unavailable/error results.

## Acceptance

For two genuinely heterogeneous pool fixtures, MarketState at beginning, intra-block, end-of-block
and range-end equals direct T040/T041 prefix reconstruction and is byte-identical for two runs that
reference the same dataset (test, golden). Storage inspection proves those runs share one canonical
market-event timeline and no per-run complete event copy exists.

For both fixtures, captured original T061 state at entry, in-range fee accrual, out-of-range wait,
rebalance and exit equals RunState position, integer inventory, ledger, equity and T052 attribution;
repeated reads are byte-equivalent (test, golden). Same-block multiple transitions are applied by
cursor then ordinal, and queries return state after every transition at that cursor.

Sparse checkpoint plus intervening transition replay equals a complete captured state without one
snapshot per cursor. Replacing or removing the installed strategy after publication changes no
RunState or ReplayFrame byte and reaches no callback. The T109 A/B/C delayed-fill fixture matches
RunState at all three cursors, including pre-fill B and post-fill C.

T104 queries at each cursor preserve dataset/window/reconstruction provenance and exact-equivalence
behavior. An inspection test proves T109, T110 and Web readers contain no second fee-growth or
range-fee implementation. Binding/tamper matrices cover dataset, PoolKey, range, cursor, revision,
transition, checkpoint and checksum mismatches plus legacy/failed/cancelled/incomplete inputs.

## Must not

rerun strategy code; duplicate the complete market timeline; implement a second replay, backtest,
accounting or fee path; infer missing evidence; require a snapshot at every cursor; use current
chain state, interpolation or a later checkpoint as historical truth; reinterpret a legacy run;
or grant execution, approval, risk, signer or live authority.
