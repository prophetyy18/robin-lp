# T109 — Publish causally bound strategy-run evidence

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `OWNER_BOOTSTRAP_SPLIT_2026_09_21`
>
> Bootstrap scope note: the Owner split the former T109 contract after attempt 2 exhausted
> both 120-turn Developer sessions. This task retains the active implementation work for causal
> scheduling, evidence, the current manifest/report authority and atomic publication. T110 owns
> qualified historical state projection and replay-frame composition; T111 owns approved research
> consumer cutover. The split changes no product requirement and removes no acceptance obligation.

## Dependencies

T041, T052, T061, T069, T100, T104, T105

## Outcome

Every new successful product backtest publishes one immutable dataset-referenced manifest, report
and simulation-evidence set whose transitions were bound to exact canonical market cursors during
the original causal T061 run. A predecessor-format success path is no longer reachable, while
historical predecessor artifacts remain byte-identical, readable as final reports and explicitly
unavailable for exact run-state replay.

## Deliverables

Define one canonical `MarketCursor` ordered as `(block_number, transaction_index, log_index)`, with
a block-only request meaning end-of-block. Extend the T061 audit chain so every decision, risk,
latency, fill and state checkpoint is bound at emission time to its exact cursor and to a globally
strictly increasing `run_transition_ordinal`. A state-changing transition with no exact cursor,
duplicate/non-increasing ordinal, or decreasing cursor order fails publication. Integer timestamps
are not cursor substitutes and a non-causal audit history is never post-sorted into validity.

Correct the single existing T061 schedule for delayed execution. A decision whose fill data comes
from a later cursor queues its latency/fill pipeline until that market event is admitted to the
information frontier. At the fill cursor, release due pipelines in deterministic trigger-cursor
and pipeline-identity order before invoking the new reactive callback at that cursor. Immediate
fills retain decision → risk → latency → fill at one cursor. No intervening callback, risk
input, metric input, ledger or accounting view may observe a future fill.

Extend the successful T069 publication transaction with a versioned, checksummed
simulation-evidence artifact. It binds run identity, immutable T100 dataset content hash and
partition references, PoolKey, range, registered strategy and parameter schema, and engine,
accounting, reconstruction and evidence-schema revisions. It contains the ordered run-specific
facts and sparse checkpoints needed by T110, but never a complete market-event copy or a snapshot
at every cursor.

Take over the current T069 product-run obligations: durable identity, queue/progress/status,
restart recovery, named failure and cancellation reasons, CLI start/list/observe/cancel, linked
reruns and atomic successful publication. Take over the current T105 manifest/report obligations:
registry/schema/code-bound strategy identity, dataset/pool/range/units/assumptions/revisions,
per-pool publication, `RELATIVE_ONLY`, checksum/tamper rejection, historical T063 compatibility and
deterministic stateless artifact rerun.

For every new write, replace the embedded complete `input_event_list` with the immutable T100
dataset content hash and partition references resolving the same canonical bytes. Publish manifest,
report and simulation evidence atomically or publish none as successful. Failed, cancelled,
incomplete or evidence-invalid runs publish no complete-looking result. `rerun-manifest` remains a
stateless artifact operation and may not invoke a current strategy to reconstruct historical state.

Preserve historical T069/T105 records byte-for-byte and read-only. A legacy successful report stays
readable, but its exact `RunState`/`ReplayFrame` capability is explicitly unavailable; it is never
regenerated, upgraded, reordered or backfilled under the new schedule. The new schema exposes the
validated dataset/evidence bindings T110 and T111 consume without granting strategy, risk,
execution, approval, signer or live authority.

## Replacement and migration

- **Current authority:** T109 remains the successor to T069 and T105 for product-run lifecycle and
  manifest/report/evidence publication. No CLI, Web, background process or test helper may publish
  a current predecessor-format success.
- **Historical artifacts:** legacy records and reports remain immutable and readable only through
  the versioned legacy reader. Exact replay is explicitly unavailable for them.
- **Runtime cutover:** queued/running records survive restart without duplication. If evidence or
  any binding cannot be written and validated, successful publication fails closed.
- **Downstream split:** T110 consumes T109 evidence and owns qualified `MarketState`, `RunState` and
  `ReplayFrame`; T111 consumes T110 and owns T101/T102/T106 compatibility and old-consumer-path
  removal. Existing consumers needing only the current manifest/evidence authority continue to
  depend on T109.
- **Security:** artifacts contain no credential, endpoint secret, signing payload or key material.

## Acceptance

Tests against the real T061 engine prove exact cursor binding and strictly increasing ordinals for
decision → risk → latency → fill, same-cursor multi-transition ordering, and rejection of
missing, duplicate, inconsistent or timestamp-only bindings. A three-cursor delayed-execution
fixture has trigger A, intervening reactive event B and fill-data event C: the actual strategy/risk/
metric/accounting inputs at B are pre-fill; C admits its market event, applies the due fill, then
invokes C's callback; the emitted audit stream is already cursor-monotonic without post-sorting.
Immediate, delayed, failure and partial-fill guarantees inherited from T061 remain passing.

Successful orchestration writes manifest, report and checksummed simulation evidence as one
publication unit. Tests kill or inject failure at every publication boundary and prove that no
partial unit can become `SUCCEEDED`; failed and cancelled runs publish neither a complete-looking
result nor replayable evidence. New manifests contain immutable dataset references and event count
but no complete input-event list, and referenced bytes/hash/range/pool/schema/revision mismatches
fail with named reasons.

All T069 lifecycle, restart, cancellation, failure, CLI, registry, dataset-range and linked-rerun
tests and all T105 identity, provenance, `RELATIVE_ONLY`, checksum, legacy, tamper and artifact-rerun
tests remain passing through the T109 authority. A structural integration test proves every current
success entry reaches T109 publication and neither predecessor publisher remains callable for new
writes. Legacy reports remain byte-identical and return an explicit replay-unavailable result.

Evidence-schema unit tests prove deterministic checksums, sparse checkpoint/transition validation,
no complete market-event serialization, and rejection of dataset, PoolKey, range, cursor, revision,
transition, ordinal and checksum tampering. These tests do not claim T110's reconstruction/golden
equivalence or T111's approved-consumer migration.

## Must not

apply a delayed fill before its actual cursor; post-sort a non-causal audit history; infer an exact
cursor from integer time; embed the complete market timeline; publish partial evidence as success;
leave a predecessor-format current success path reachable; mutate or regenerate historical
artifacts; create a second runner, manifest authority, accounting engine or fee projection; invoke
strategy code for artifact rerun; grant execution, approval, risk or live authority; or serialize
credentials, endpoint secrets or key material.
