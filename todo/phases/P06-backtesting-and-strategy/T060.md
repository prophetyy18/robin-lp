# T060 — Define strategy contracts

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T050, T051, T053

## Components, outputs and the model extension point

Component names follow `docs/spec/strategy/STRATEGY_ECONOMICS.md` §7: `RegimeModel` and
`FeeOpportunityModel` are the component names — the substitutable interface
implementations. `RegimeAssessment` and the fee-opportunity assessment are the
corresponding output names — the versioned and unit-carrying values those components
return. A component name never denotes an output, and an output name never denotes a
component.

The regime and fee-opportunity components are the sole supported extension point for a
trained model. A model-backed implementation must satisfy the same contract as a rule
implementation — the same versioned point-in-time inputs, output names, shapes and
units, and the same validation and determinism duties — and may not alter the
admission/market/portfolio snapshots, the candidate action, the ledger, risk or audit
semantics.

Each component interface must be able to signal uncertainty and refuse to produce an
assessment rather than force one: a component without the evidence to assess returns an
explicit uncertainty outcome, and no assessment is fabricated in its place.

## Outcome

strategy logic is a pure consumer of timestamped state.

## Deliverables

immutable, versioned admission/market/portfolio snapshots;
the `RegimeModel` and `FeeOpportunityModel` components with their `RegimeAssessment`
and fee-opportunity assessment outputs, and the candidate action; deterministic
clock/random seed; tick/capital proposal validation; reason-coded `NO_TRADE`.

## Acceptance

dependency tests prevent RPC/storage/signing/execution imports;
one instance works unchanged on heterogeneous eligible PoolKeys; invalid ticks,
stale state, NaN/display values, and excessive capital fail before intent creation;
a component without the evidence to assess returns an explicit uncertainty outcome
rather than a forced or defaulted assessment.

## Must not

let strategy approve its own risk or mutate ledger/state directly; let a component
alter the snapshots, the candidate action, the ledger, risk or audit semantics, or
force an assessment the interface cannot support.
