# T105 — Bind experiment manifests and reports to the strategy registry

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `OWNER_AMENDMENT_A0014`

## Dependencies

T063, T068

## Outcome

every published result remains reproducible and auditable, and its strategy identity and
parameters are bound to the exact versioned registry entry that authorized the run.

## Deliverables

Extend the T063 manifest and report path without weakening any T063 obligation. The manifest
still records dataset/schema/code/dependency revisions, chain, exactly one PoolKey and that
pool's block range and interval, seed, clock/fill/cost/quote assumptions, dataset version,
reporting numeraire and valuation qualification, units for every recorded quantity, decisions,
ledger, metrics and report checksums. A multi-pool run remains one run identity over one dataset
version and reporting numeraire with one manifest per member pool.

Replace the hard-coded strategy-name vocabulary with T068's registry authority. Each manifest
records the stable registered strategy identity, strategy version, registry revision and
checksum, the applicable parameter-schema identity/version/checksum, validated parameter values
and registered code provenance. Validation rejects an unregistered identity, a parameter absent
from the schema, a value outside its declared type/unit/range, or a registry/schema/code binding
that disagrees with the recorded revision. The canonical manifest bytes and checksums bind all of
those fields.

Keep T063's `rerun-manifest` boundary explicit: it verifies and deterministically reproduces an
already saved manifest as an artifact operation. T069 owns creation and durable lifecycle of a
product run—run identity, queue/progress/status, failure, cancellation and restart. A rerun that
is requested through the product path creates a new T069 run record linked to the source manifest;
it never mutates the source manifest or silently acquires T069 state inside the artifact command.

## Replacement and migration

- **Old code reachability:** adapt or replace the current `robinhood_lp.reports` manifest,
  validation, run-identity and rerun entry points and the `rerun-manifest` CLI integration. After
  cutover, no production, CLI, Web, background or test helper may publish a current manifest via
  `VALID_STRATEGY_KINDS` or another caller-supplied/free-text identity list. The old validator may
  exist only as unreachable Git history or as an explicitly versioned legacy reader.
- **Historical data and artifacts:** preserve every T063 manifest/report/checksum and review
  artifact byte-for-byte and read-only. A legacy manifest may be inspected or rerun only under its
  recorded legacy schema with an explicit legacy marker; it is never rewritten to claim a registry
  revision it did not record and cannot serve as current promotion evidence until migrated by a
  deterministic, additive derived artifact that retains the source checksum and provenance.
- **Runtime cutover and rollback/fail closed:** cut over manifest creation atomically after the
  required registry revision is available. Missing, unknown or mismatched registry/schema/code
  bindings fail before publication. Rollback disables new publication or restores the last known
  compatible registry-bound release; it never re-enables hard-coded or inferred identities.
- **Downstream dependencies:** later CONTRACT amendments must remove T063 from all ordinary
  `PLANNED` consumers. T069, T073, T084 and T101 must consume T105; T068 must cease consuming the
  manifest it supplies identity to, avoiding a T068↔T105 cycle. T106 and T107 consume T105.
- **Security:** registry data is non-secret and deterministic; manifests still exclude credentials,
  endpoint secrets and key material. Registry binding grants no execution, risk, approval or live
  authority.
- **Verification:** add positive coverage for every registered strategy including T065, per-pool
  and multi-pool manifests and both artifact rerun and T069-linked rerun; negative coverage for
  unregistered identity, undeclared/out-of-range/wrong-unit parameters, checksum/revision/code
  mismatch and attempted legacy publication; compatibility and migration fixtures for historical
  T063 artifacts; and an old-path-unreachable test proving current publication cannot consult the
  hard-coded vocabulary.

## Acceptance

All T063 reconciliation, tamper, numeraire, unit, per-pool and multi-pool acceptance remains
passing. A registered T065 strategy produces a manifest whose identity, parameters, schema,
registry revision and code provenance validate and whose canonical rerun reproduces decisions,
ledger and metrics. Every invalid binding listed above fails before publication. Historical T063
artifacts remain readable and byte-identical but are visibly legacy and cannot be mistaken for
registry-bound evidence. The T063 artifact command and T069 product lifecycle boundary is covered
by tests, and no reachable current publication path uses the hard-coded identity set.

## Must not

overwrite a prior run or historical artifact; publish a chart without manifest/coverage; publish
without dataset version or reporting numeraire; present `RELATIVE_ONLY` as USD-denominated; infer,
substitute or accept a strategy identity or parameter outside T068; make Web state part of the
artifact rerun command; or give a manifest execution, approval or promotion authority.
