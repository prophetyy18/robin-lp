# T111 — Cut approved research consumers over to replay artifacts

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `OWNER_BOOTSTRAP_SPLIT_2026_09_21`

## Dependencies

T101, T102, T106, T110

## Outcome

Approved T101, T102 and T106 consumers accept current T112 dataset-referenced artifacts and paired
simulation-evidence through one validated compatibility view, reject identity or byte mismatches,
and cannot reach predecessor T069/T105 publication or reconstruct run state with current strategy
code. T109 is preserved only as a historical read-only predecessor and is not a current
evidence source.

## Deliverables

Provide one versioned compatibility adapter from a T112 manifest and paired simulation-evidence
(bound to immutable T100 partition references) to the logical run/dataset/pool/range/registry/
schema identity and canonical ordered event bytes previously consumed by T101 and T106. The
adapter additionally preserves historical-only compatibility for legacy readers of a T109
manifest through the versioned legacy reader; a T109-versioned record is never accepted as current
T112 evidence. T102 continues through those approved surfaces. The adapter invokes neither the
T105 publisher nor a strategy callback and creates no second manifest, replay, backtest or
accounting authority.

Preserve every logical field required by the approved consumer contracts. If any field, byte,
revision, checksum or identity cannot be preserved exactly, fail closed instead of translating,
defaulting or weakening it. Keep historical T105 and T109 artifacts on their versioned read-only
path; the adapter applies only to current T112 artifacts.

Remove every current T101/T102/T106 route to the predecessor publication shape. Document and test
the migration boundary so future callers choose the current adapter explicitly and cannot treat a
legacy final report as replayable evidence.

## Acceptance

Integration tests drive the real T101 panel/harness and T106 robustness runner with two current
T112 artifacts and prove they receive the same logical identities and canonical ordered event bytes
as their approved contracts require. T102 completes its T101/T106 integration unchanged in
meaning. Dataset reference/hash, range, PoolKey, registry/schema and revision mismatches fail with
named reasons; a T109-versioned record offered as current is rejected with a named reason.

Call-graph/architecture tests prove no current consumer reaches the T069/T105 publishers, no
consumer invokes a strategy to reconstruct a completed run, and only the T112/T110 authorities
resolve manifest/evidence/state. Legacy fixtures (including T109 historical artifacts) remain
final-report-readable but explicitly unavailable to the current adapter and replay surfaces.

All approved T101, T102 and T106 regression suites remain passing. A deterministic rerun from the
pinned dataset bytes is byte-equivalent and does not mutate its source manifest or evidence.

## Must not

change an approved consumer's logical schema; publish through T069/T105; copy the full market
timeline into a new artifact; invoke current strategy code to rebuild historical state; silently
upgrade legacy artifacts; weaken registry/schema/hash/revision checks; or grant execution, risk,
approval, signer or live authority.
