# T106 — Constrain robustness analysis to registered parameter schemas

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `OWNER_AMENDMENT_A0014`

## Dependencies

T064, T068, T105

## Outcome

favorable parameters are challenged outside their selection sample only across the parameter
surface the selected registered strategy actually declares.

## Deliverables

Preserve T064's anchored/rolling walk-forward splits, untouched holdout, pool/regime segmentation,
scenario catalogue, multiple-comparison disclosure, pool holdout as the primary generalisation
test, time holdout and walk-forward evidence, and the single label-horizon-derived purge/embargo
gap with its unit. Preserve the predeclared halt-or-degrade result for every stressed gas,
latency, slippage, fee, missing-data and reorg scenario.

Build each parameter-surface axis from the exact T068 registered identity and parameter-schema
revision recorded by the T105 manifest. Axis names, types, units and values must be declared by the
schema and remain inside its inclusive/exclusive ranges and other constraints. Record the registry
and schema identity, version and checksum with the surface and robustness report. Reject rather
than clip, coerce, ignore or publish any undeclared, wrong-unit, wrong-type or out-of-range point.
A registry/schema change produces a separately identified surface and report; results from
incompatible schema revisions are not merged into one sensitivity conclusion.

## Replacement and migration

- **Old code reachability:** adapt or replace `robinhood_lp.robustness.surfaces`, runner and report
  construction so every current parameter surface enters through registry-schema validation. Any
  generic legacy surface builder remains available only to an explicit legacy-reader/test path and
  cannot publish current robustness evidence.
- **Historical data and artifacts:** preserve T064 split, scenario, surface and report artifacts
  read-only with their original checksums. Mark artifacts without registry/schema binding as legacy;
  do not rewrite or silently infer a schema. A derived migration artifact retains the source
  checksum and records the exact compatible registry revision or refuses migration.
- **Runtime cutover and rollback/fail closed:** activate schema-bound construction only after T068
  and T105 are available. Unknown or unavailable revisions, invalid points and incompatible stored
  surfaces fail before evaluation/publication. Rollback pauses current robustness publication at
  the last compatible schema rather than reopening unrestricted surfaces.
- **Downstream dependencies:** T107 consumes T106. T102 must depend on and cite T106 as the current
  schema-bound robustness protocol instead of treating T064 as the current authority; its model
  evaluation must consume T106 evidence without reopening the unrestricted T064 surface. T096 must
  carry T106's independently reviewed evidence into the final matrix. T064 remains only as T106's
  declared replacement dependency, immutable history and an explicitly versioned legacy reader.
- **Security:** schema validation is deterministic and local, imports no RPC, Web, signer or
  execution authority, and accepts no free-text identity as authorization.
- **Verification:** retain T064 positive, adverse-scenario and anti-look-ahead tests; add boundary
  tests at every declared range edge, negative undeclared/out-of-range/type/unit/revision cases,
  compatibility and migration fixtures for T064 artifacts, and an old-path-unreachable test proving
  current publication cannot use an unvalidated generic surface.

## Acceptance

Every T064 acceptance condition remains passing. For at least two heterogeneous registered
strategies, every evaluated surface point is schema-declared and within range, the report binds the
same registry/schema revision as its T105 manifest, and repeated construction is deterministic.
Invalid points fail before any fold is evaluated or report is written. Historical T064 artifacts
remain readable and byte-identical with an explicit legacy status, and current publication cannot
reach the unrestricted surface path.

## Must not

retune on test, discard losing pools/regimes post hoc, rank solely by annualized return, present a
time-only holdout as primary, hand-pick the purge/embargo gap, extrapolate beyond registered ranges,
clip or coerce invalid points, infer schema from implementation signatures, or merge incompatible
registry revisions.
