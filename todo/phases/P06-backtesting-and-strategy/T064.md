# T064 — Add robustness and anti-overfitting analysis

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T063

## Outcome

favorable parameters are challenged outside their selection sample.

## Deliverables

anchored/rolling walk-forward splits, untouched holdout, parameter
surfaces, pool/regime segmentation, stressed gas/latency/slippage/fees, missing-
data and reorg scenarios, and multiple-comparison disclosure; pool holdout as the
primary generalisation test, with time holdout and walk-forward retained beside it;
and a purge and embargo gap between training and evaluation folds whose length is
derived from the declared label horizon, recorded with the split and stated in the
horizon's own unit (for example bars or blocks).

## Acceptance

split boundaries are point-in-time and machine recorded; reports
prominently separate train/validation/test; failure scenarios halt or degrade as
specified; conclusions include sensitivity, not only best run; the purge and embargo
length is derivable from the declared label horizon rather than hand-chosen and is
recorded with each split; pool-holdout results are reported per held-out pool and are
the primary generalisation statement, because a time-only holdout keeps the same
market regimes on both sides.

## Must not

retune on test, discard losing pools/regimes post hoc, or rank solely
by annualized return; or present a time-only holdout as the primary generalisation
test, or choose a purge or embargo length by hand.
