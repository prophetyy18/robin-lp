# T064 — Add robustness and anti-overfitting analysis

> Phase: `P06`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T063

## Outcome

favorable parameters are challenged outside their selection sample.

## Deliverables

anchored/rolling walk-forward splits, untouched holdout, parameter
surfaces, pool/regime segmentation, a scenario catalogue covering the stressed
gas/latency/slippage/fee cases and the missing-data and reorg cases, and
multiple-comparison disclosure; pool holdout as the primary generalisation test, with
time holdout and walk-forward retained beside it; and one purge/embargo gap between
training and evaluation folds — a single length, not two — whose length equals the
declared label horizon and is derived from it, recorded with the split and stated in the
horizon's own unit (for example bars or blocks); every catalogue entry declares the
halt-or-degrade outcome `DS-035` requires before the run, rather than leaving it to be
inferred.

## Acceptance

split boundaries are point-in-time and machine recorded; reports
prominently separate train/validation/test; each failure scenario halts or degrades
exactly as its catalogue entry declares (`DS-035`), and no degrade falls through to
undefined behaviour or a forced trade (`DS-041`); conclusions include sensitivity, not
only best run; the single purge/embargo gap length is derived from the declared label
horizon — equal to it — rather than hand-chosen and is recorded with each split;
pool-holdout results are reported per held-out pool and are the primary generalisation
statement, because a time-only holdout keeps the same market regimes on both sides.

## Must not

retune on test, discard losing pools/regimes post hoc, or rank solely
by annualized return; or present a time-only holdout as the primary generalisation
test, or choose a purge or embargo length by hand.
