# T096 — Produce final V1 acceptance and traceability dossier

> Phase: `P09`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T073, T087, T088, T095, T097, T108

## Outcome

the owner can determine whether every V1 goal is met from linked evidence.

## Deliverables

goal → spec → task → test/evidence matrix; clean install/deploy/operate/
backup/restore runbook; supported identities/limits; research, paper, testnet, security and
mainnet manifests; known limitations and incident history.

The matrix covers the plan this gate now governs and names each delivery with the goals it
carries instead of relying on an unnamed transitive path. The registry-bound deliveries are
named with their current goals and specification clauses: T105 (the registry-bound manifest
and reports, and the artifact-versus-product rerun boundary, behind `G-STRATEGY-REGISTRY-01`
and `G-BACKTEST-RUN-01`, with the `docs/spec/research/DATASET_AND_EVALUATION.md` obligations
it preserves), T106 (the schema-bound robustness surfaces and reports that `DS-021`,
`DS-022`, `DS-035` and `DS-041` require), T107 (the registry-bound candidate lock and search
surface, and the frozen identity, schema and registry revision, behind
`G-STRATEGY-REGISTRY-01`, with `docs/spec/strategy/STRATEGY_ECONOMICS.md` section 6 and
`DS-031`), and T108 (the current authoring guide, worked example and drift tests that keep
`G-STRATEGY-REGISTRY-01` teachable, with `docs/spec/strategy/STRATEGY_ECONOMICS.md` section
7). The A0012-era deliveries whose goals this gate already claims to check are named the same
way: T068 (the strategy registry behind `G-STRATEGY-REGISTRY-01`), T069 (the product-level
run entry point and durable run lifecycle behind `G-BACKTEST-RUN-01`), T073 (the pre-paper
authorization entry behind `G-PAPER-AUTHORIZE-01` and `G-PERM-WRITE-01`, with
`WEB-GLOBAL-006`), T087 (the registry and run page whose coverage provenance `WEB-PAGE-012`
requires), T088 (the backtest result page whose layering, attribution residual and coverage
rendering `G-BACKTEST-RESULT-01`, `WEB-PAGE-013` and `WEB-GLOBAL-007` require), and T097
(the read-only and reduce-only CLI control surface behind `G-PERM-WRITE-01`, `G-EXIT-01` and
`G-CONTROL-01`, with `CTRL-CLI-001`). Each of T105, T106, T107 and T108 is mapped to its own
independently reviewed evidence rather than to the predecessor's approval, review record or
artifact, and every other `G-*`, `ADM-*`, `WEB-*`, `ECO-*` and `CTRL-*` obligation maps to
its delivering task and to that task's own independently reviewed evidence the same way.

The gate's dependency closure contains each named delivery: T073, T087, T088, T097 and T108
by direct dependency; T068 through T087, T088 and T108, and T069 through the product-run
dependency that T087 and T088 each carry; T105 through T108 and T073; T107 through T073; and
T106 through T107. No named delivery is reached only by an unnamed path, and the closure is
recomputed rather than assumed whenever the graph changes.

Each claim resolves to the current artifact of the delivery it names — the registry-bound
manifest and reports, the schema-bound robustness surface and report, the registry-bound
candidate lock, and the current guide, example and drift tests — and never to a predecessor
artifact. Predecessor records (a `T063` manifest, a `T064` surface or report, a `T066` lock,
a `T067` guide) appear only as linked, read-only history: they are not rewritten, re-derived
or backfilled, and no goal is marked met on their authority. A goal whose required delivery
is absent, failed, cancelled, legacy-only or not independently approved stays unmet with the
reason recorded; no plan-level index entry, task checkbox or predecessor approval stands in
for the delivery's own reviewed evidence.

T096 keeps two authority distinctions the registry work does not change: T108's registration
guidance and worked example are documentation and grant no permission to register,
parameterise, publish, run, trade or promote; and no artifact of T105, T106 or T107 —
manifest, robustness report or candidate lock — carries execution, approval, paper or live
authority, so none of them can satisfy T094 by itself.

## Acceptance

every `G-*`, `ADM-*`, `WEB-*`, `ECO-*` and `CTRL-*` maps to passing evidence or
an explicit blocker; clean-room reproduction succeeds; all required blockers are closed; a
test asserts that the configured dependency closure of T096 contains T068, T069, T073, T087,
T088, T097, T105, T106, T107 and T108, and that the matrix names each of them with the goals
it carries (test); each named delivery resolves to its current artifact — the registry-bound
manifest, the schema-bound robustness report, the registry-bound candidate lock, and the
current guide, example and drift tests — and the matrix records that delivery's positive,
negative, compatibility, migration and old-path-unreachable evidence, including the T108
guide, example and drift tests (test); a predecessor artifact, a legacy-only delivery, a
delivery that is failed, cancelled or not independently approved, and an old-path artifact
offered as current each leave their goal unmet with the reason recorded instead of passing
(test, fixture); predecessor manifests, surfaces, locks, guides and review records stay
linked and byte-identical, and no matrix entry rewrites or re-derives one (fixture, test);
and T096 claims no permission from T108's guidance and no execution, approval, paper or live
authority from a T105, T106 or T107 artifact (test).

## Must not

use task checkmarks without evidence, omit failed experiments/incidents, or claim
profitability when the acceptance standard is safety/correctness; or count a predecessor
artifact, approval or record as current evidence for any goal; or mark a goal met through a
delivery that is absent, failed, cancelled, legacy-only or not independently approved; or
read T108's registration guidance or example as a permission, or a T105 manifest, T106 report
or T107 candidate lock as execution, approval, paper or live authority; or rewrite, re-derive
or backfill a predecessor artifact or review record.
