# T071 — Implement paper execution and immutable ledger

> Phase: `P07`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T061, T070, T073

## Outcome

simulated balances/positions reconcile through realistic failures.

Paper operation is gated by `G-PAPER-AUTHORIZE-01`: the active-`PoolKey` selection, both
tokens' `HOLD`/`LP` approvals and the preliminary-paper authorization must be completed
by the Owner through the Web entry point T073 delivers and recorded as immutable versions
with their audit events. While T073 is not `APPROVED`, or while any of the three records
is absent, superseded or not bound to the active pool and strategy version, this task
starts no paper run and changes no simulated balance.

## Deliverables

intents, approvals, fills, liquidity changes, fee collection, gas,
failure/retry and balances as append-only double-entry-style audit events.

The paper-run gate is part of the delivery: a run request is refused with the missing
prerequisite named, and no ledger event is written, until the three T073 records exist for
the active pool and strategy version.

## Acceptance

backtest and paper share strategy/risk interfaces; every balance
change links to an event; duplicate/reordered/crash recovery is idempotent; daily
opening + flows + PnL = closing equity; no paper run starts, and no
simulated balance or position changes, before the active-`PoolKey` selection, the two
tokens' `HOLD`/`LP` approvals and the preliminary-paper authorization exist as immutable
records written through T073's entry point, and an absent, superseded or mismatched record
refuses the run and records what is missing rather than starting it (test).

## Must not

mutate balances without a ledger event, call `eth_sendRawTransaction`,
label a simulated receipt as on-chain, operate a paper run before T073 is `APPROVED` and
the three authorizations it writes exist for the active pool and strategy version, or
treat an absent or superseded authorization as a warning rather than a refusal.
