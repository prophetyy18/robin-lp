# T072 — Add real-time read-only ingestion and recovery

> Phase: `P07`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T033, T071, T073

## Outcome

backfill reaches a safe head before decisions, then follows without gaps.

Ingestion may backfill, catch up and follow before the paper authorizations exist, but
decision resume does not: under `G-PAPER-AUTHORIZE-01` the active-`PoolKey` selection,
both tokens' `HOLD`/`LP` approvals and the preliminary-paper authorization must be
completed by the Owner through T073's Web entry point before this task operates paper.
Decisions therefore resume only once those three records exist for the active pool and
strategy version, in addition to the completeness and freshness gates below; while any of
them is absent or superseded, the decision stream stays paused and names what is missing.

## Deliverables

backfill→catch-up→follow state machine, polling/subscription adapter,
watermarks, reconnect/common-ancestor recovery, decision pause/resume, health state.

The resume gate covers the authorization prerequisite as well as data completeness: the
pause/resume surface refuses to resume decisions, and records the reason, until T073 is
`APPROVED` and the three authorization records exist for the active pool and strategy
version.

## Acceptance

disconnect, dropped/duplicate/out-of-order notifications, restart,
provider switch, shallow/deep reorg and head-stall tests create neither gaps nor
duplicate decisions; decisions resume only after completeness/freshness gates pass;
decisions do not resume while T073 is unapproved or while any of the three authorization
records is absent, superseded or bound to a different pool or strategy version, and that
pause is reported with the missing prerequisite named rather than the decision stream
continuing (test).

## Must not

depend on remote filter survival, make decisions while catching up, or resume decisions
while a `G-PAPER-AUTHORIZE-01` authorization is absent, superseded or not bound to the
active pool and strategy version.
