# T073 — Deliver the pre-paper authorization entry

> Phase: `P07`
> Contract status: `OWNER_PLAN_EXTENSION_2026-09-20`

## Dependencies

T025, T027, T105, T107

## Outcome

before any preliminary-paper run, the Owner completes the active-pool selection, the
`HOLD`/`LP` approvals and the preliminary-paper authorization through one bounded Web
entry point that writes immutable versioned records, so which entry point carries those
three decisions is stated rather than left to the later console.

The actor is the Owner. No other role, no background process and no CLI performs these
three journeys, and the entry point must be reachable before T071 and T072 operate paper.

## Deliverables

a bounded, single-owner, authenticated local Web entry point offering exactly three write
journeys: selecting or switching the one active `PoolKey` under single-select semantics;
approving or adjusting the target and pair tokens' `HOLD`/`LP` permissions and their USDG
exposure limits; and recording the preliminary-paper authorization, which binds the active
pool, the active strategy version, the paper environment and the authorized scope.

Each journey uses the write path `WEB-GLOBAL-003` requires — draft, impact preview,
reauthentication, explicit confirmation, then an immutable version and audit event — and
records actor, reason and evidence for every accepted and every rejected write. The entry
point reads the T025 admission records and the T027 classification as its inputs instead
of restating eligibility rules, and shows the registry-bound evidence the authorization
refers to: the current T105 manifest and reports, and the current T107 candidate lock with
the registered strategy identity, strategy version, registry revision and parameter-schema
identity it freezes. Those views read current registry-bound artifacts only: a T063
manifest or a T066 lock appears, if at all, as an explicitly historical, non-current
artifact, is never copied into a new authorization, and an absent, mismatched or
downgraded current manifest or candidate lock fails closed instead of authorizing. It is
bounded: it delivers neither the P08 console shell (T084) nor any of the four research
pages T103 owns, and it exposes no live promotion.

## Acceptance

each of the three journeys writes an immutable versioned record carrying actor, reason and
evidence, and the preliminary-paper authorization names the active `PoolKey`, the strategy
version and the authorized scope (test); a replayed submit, an expired session, a
conflicting version and a duplicate confirmation fail closed and write nothing (test); a
request that would increase a permission or an exposure creates a new version and
invalidates the affected authorization, while a reduction takes effect immediately and is
never blocked by an increase-risk flow (test); an unapproved token, a pool that fails its
technical gate and a strategy version with no backtest evidence behind it are refused with
the reason recorded rather than written (test); the evidence and candidate views display
the current T105 manifest and the current T107 candidate lock with the registered strategy
identity and the registry/schema revision they record, while a legacy T063 manifest or
T066 lock is rendered as explicitly historical and non-current (test); an absent current
manifest or candidate lock, a content-hash or registry/schema revision mismatch, and a
downgrade attempt to the legacy format each fail closed and authorize nothing, with the
reason recorded (test); the entry point accepts, echoes or persists no private key,
Keystore password, seed or RPC credential (test); and the P07 phase entry gate and this
contract name the same entry point as the one that carries the preliminary-paper
authorization (inspection of `todo/phases/P07-risk-and-paper/README.md` and this contract).

## Must not

accept a private key, Keystore password, seed or RPC credential; expose a generic
transaction or arbitrary-call form; promote live or raise a limit outside the authorized
scope; present preliminary paper as a graded live-promotion step; implement the P08 console
shell or a T103 research page; treat a legacy T063 manifest or T066 candidate lock as
current authorization evidence, or accept an absent, mismatched or downgraded current
manifest or candidate lock as one; or let a CLI, a background process or a background
service perform any of the three journeys in the Owner's place.
