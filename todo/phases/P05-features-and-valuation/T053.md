# T053 — Add point-in-time quote and gas valuation

> Phase: `P05`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T034, T049

## Reporting numeraire

The quote layer applies the ADR-014 reporting-numeraire hierarchy instead of a
USDG-only source, and records per dataset which level was selected and why:

- **USDG** whenever the dataset contains USDG and a qualified valuation exists for it;
- otherwise a **qualified USD stablecoin** that passed the same qualification as USDG;
- **ETH** for display only, explicitly labelled as a volatile numeraire, never a
  primary benchmark and never a basis for ranking across numeraires.

A dataset with neither a USD-family asset nor a qualified conversion route is reported
`RELATIVE_ONLY` and carries relative quantities only (token1 per token0). Such a
dataset contains no USD-denominated field anywhere — no PnL, value, fee, gas or risk
quantity — and its results are never ranked against USD-denominated results.

The execution path is unchanged by this generalisation: the active pool's valuation,
exposure and risk budget, and both 5-minute extreme-move rules, still require a
qualified point-in-time USDG conversion, and USDG remains mandatory there whichever
numeraire a research dataset reports.

Observed-at/available-at, source, pair, confidence and staleness semantics are
unchanged, and they must hold for whichever numeraire is selected rather than only for
USDG. Every quoted quantity states its unit (raw token integer, numeraire units, ratio,
dimensionless) and the time at which it became available.

## Outcome

cross-token performance never uses future or unverifiable prices.

## Deliverables

provider-neutral observation schema with observed-at/available-at,
source, pair, block/time, confidence/staleness; conversion graph and missing policy;
qualified point-in-time observations in the dataset's reporting numeraire — USDG on the
execution path — and complete bars usable by strategy and risk without importing
non-market fundamental signals; a per-numeraire qualification record, and a
`RELATIVE_ONLY` path whose output carries no USD-denominated field.

## Acceptance

delayed/revised/depegged/missing/cross-rate fixtures are tested;
prefix invariance holds; the same versioned point-in-time USDG price semantics feed
performance, exposure and both 5-minute extreme-move rules; reports can remain in raw units;
each numeraire level, including `RELATIVE_ONLY`, has its own fixtures, and a
`RELATIVE_ONLY` output contains no USD-denominated field and is not ranked against a
USD-denominated result; availability time, provenance and staleness are asserted per
numeraire rather than only for USDG.

## Must not

assume a stablecoin equals one dollar, silently use current prices, or
mix sources/frequencies without provenance; or convert a `RELATIVE_ONLY` dataset into
USD terms, or treat an ETH display value as a primary benchmark.
