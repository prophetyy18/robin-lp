# T050 — Add time/block bars and market features

> Phase: `P05`
> Contract status: `FROZEN_FROM_BASELINE_6C31778`

## Dependencies

T042

## Ownership boundary

Time and block bars and the market features derived from them — volume, realized
volatility, price range, active liquidity, depth proxy, effective fee, gas and
freshness — belong to this task. The forward-looking research panel labels belong to
T101: the label horizon, the moment each label becomes observable and the label
matrix are defined and produced there, and T101 consumes these features as its input.
This task neither defines, computes nor publishes labels, and no bar or market
feature may be re-derived or duplicated in the label harness — each quantity is
implemented once, in exactly one of the two tasks.

## Outcome

features expose only information available at their declared timestamp.

## Deliverables

bars for volume, realized volatility, price range, active liquidity,
depth proxy, effective fee, gas, and freshness, each carrying its unit, window, data
time and availability time; event-time/watermark contract.

## Acceptance

tests prove left/right window boundaries, late event handling, empty
windows, irregular blocks, and prefix invariance (adding future data never changes
an earlier feature row).

## Must not

use centered/global normalization or label a proxy as observed depth.
