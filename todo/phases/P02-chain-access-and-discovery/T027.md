# T027 — Classify research-universe pools and their support levels

> Phase: `P02`
> Contract status: `OWNER_PLAN_EXTENSION_2026-09-19`

## Dependencies

T023, T026

## Outcome

Every pool in the research universe carries a reason-coded classification and a
support level established on its own technical identity, independently of any target
token, so that a pool can be researched without being approved for holding or trading
and can never be mistaken for an approved one.

This contract supersedes T023: it restates that task's full obligation set and adds
research-universe classification, the level gate that makes `backtest` the entry point
for replay, backtest and model research, and the explicit separation between a
research classification and an execution approval. T023's implementation is live and
expected to be reused, cited and extended rather than rewritten; T023 itself is retired
as a governing contract so that one contract, not two, describes this area.

A research member holds nothing and trades nothing, so it requires no `HOLD`, `LP` or
`AUTO_SWAP` approval; what it does require is that ingestion capability is never
confused with simulation capability, and that the boundary between the two is recorded
rather than assumed (ADR-014).

## Deliverables

- The reason-coded classifier and its evidence model, preserved from T023:
  deployment identity, static and dynamic fee, hook flags with code hash and
  upgradeability evidence, token metadata, data coverage, modelled deltas, and the
  resulting support level.
- Classification keyed on `(chain_id, PoolKey)` alone, requiring no target token and
  consulting no token approval state.
- The `backtest` gate: a member below that level is refused for replay, backtest and
  model research, with the blocking reason surfaced rather than the member silently
  dropped or quietly included.
- An explicit, machine-readable statement that a research classification is not, and
  does not imply, a token or pool approval, and that it grants no execution authority.
- Demotion on behaviour or code-hash change, and on a hook whose settlement effect
  cannot be verified, applied to research members as well as to execution candidates.

## Acceptance

Every registry row — execution candidate and research member alike — carries a level
plus its evidence; unknown hooks and return-delta flags default to `ingestion`;
a behaviour or code-hash change demotes the pool; classification decisions are
deterministic and audited; a research member below `backtest` is refused for replay,
backtest and model research with its named reason; a member with unverifiable hook
semantics cannot be promoted beyond `ingestion`; and no classification outcome
produced for a research member carries, or can be read as carrying, execution
authority or token approval.

Boundary and failure cases covered: a pool whose metadata call fails; a hook address
whose flag bits disagree with its code hash; a dynamic-fee pool with no observed fee;
a member whose data coverage is partial; a member demoted while a research dataset
references it; and a pool classified identically under two different target tokens.

## Must not

Infer semantics from hook address flags alone; whitelist by name; fall back to
plain-pool behavior; let a research classification stand in for a token or pool
approval; classify by token symbol or by the target-token context; or promote a
research member above `ingestion` without the evidence the execution path would
require.
