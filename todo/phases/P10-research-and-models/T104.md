# T104 — Publish the per-window fee-growth surface

> Phase: `P10`
> Contract status: `OWNER_PLAN_EXTENSION_2026-09-19`

## Dependencies

T039, T041

## Outcome

For a reconstructed pool and window, the historical fee of **any** candidate range and
**any** liquidity is an O(1) query, computed from the reconstructed tick state rather
than re-simulated and never estimated from a volume aggregate.

This is the primitive the whole research stack stands on. Fees accrue per swap and are
linear in liquidity, so one pass over the reconstructed state yields prefix sums from
which every range is a lookup; without it, every range sweep, every label and every
console range view would re-integrate the event stream, and the cheap option left to a
hurried implementation would be the forbidden one — inferring fees from a share of
aggregated volume.

## Deliverables

- Per-window cumulative `feeGrowthInside` deltas published as prefix sums over
  initialized ticks, derived from the reconstructed tick state for one pool and one
  window.
- A range query returning, for `[tickLower, tickUpper]` and liquidity `L`, the exact
  historical fees over the window, honouring V4's boundary conventions for initialized
  ticks.
- A versioned artifact bound to the pool, the window, the dataset version it was
  derived from, and the reconstruction revision, so a later reader can tell which data
  produced it.
- An equivalence check that compares the surface against direct per-position
  integration over the same window, published with the artifact.
- Documented units, scaling and the `2^128` fixed-point convention, so the linearity
  identity is checkable rather than assumed.

## Acceptance

For pinned ranges spanning in-range, out-of-range, price-reversal and multi-crossing
windows, the surface reproduces **exactly** the fees obtained by integrating
`ΔfeeGrowthInside` per position over the same window; the identity
`fees(L) = L × Δ / 2^128` is verified to hold at two distinct liquidity values, so
linearity is measured and not merely asserted; a range entirely outside the traded
price path returns zero rather than a small positive residue; a range containing no
initialized tick is handled by its own named case; a window with no swaps returns zero;
and the artifact records the pool, window, dataset version and reconstruction revision
it was derived from.

Boundary and invalid-input cases covered: `tickLower == tickUpper`; a range whose
bounds sit exactly on initialized ticks; a range spanning the full tick range; a
window that begins or ends mid-price-movement; a pool whose window contains a single
swap; and a requested liquidity of zero.

## Must not

Estimate fees as share multiplied by aggregated or bucketed volume; include the
hypothetical position's own liquidity in the historical active-liquidity denominator,
or omit it from the counterfactual numerator; carry a prefix sum across pools or across
disjoint windows; treat a zero-fee window as missing or incomplete data; or publish a
surface without the equivalence check that makes it trustworthy.
