# T101 — Build the panel labels and the training/evaluation harness

> Phase: `P10`
> Contract status: `OWNER_PLAN_EXTENSION_2026-09-18`

## Dependencies

T050, T063, T100, T104

## Outcome

A reproducible, leak-free panel dataset and model harness whose models estimate the
quantities an LP decision actually needs — the probability that price leaves a
candidate range, and the forward volatility, volume, fee density and loss-versus-
rebalancing proxy that determine whether providing liquidity there is worth its
risk. Models never estimate a return directly and never emit a position.

The measured sample size is the binding constraint and the harness must state it
rather than hide it: the research window is per pool, from that pool's own `Initialize`
block to the run's agreed finalized block (ADR-015), so what one pool yields is its own
history rather than a fixed width. The pinned reference pool was initialized about
twelve days before the last probe, which at the measured 0.1008-second block interval is
roughly 3.4 thousand five-minute bars or 288 hourly bars, and a multi-pool panel is
dominated by a small number of shared market regimes. A model that cannot be
distinguished from a trivial baseline under those conditions must be reported as such.

## Deliverables

- A point-in-time feature matrix assembled from the versioned feature registry, with
  every feature carrying its unit, window, availability time and version, and with
  no feature that was unavailable at its decision timestamp.
- Forward labels over a declared horizon: realized volatility, volume, fee density,
  the loss-versus-rebalancing proxy, the probability that price exits a candidate
  range, and net LP return. A label's horizon and the moment it becomes observable
  are recorded with it.
- An explicitly named statistical boundary at which floating-point arithmetic is
  permitted, so that protocol, replay, valuation and accounting paths remain
  integer-exact and the boundary is visible rather than implied.
- Split machinery: pool holdout, time holdout and walk-forward, with a purge and an
  embargo whose length is stated and derived from the label horizon.
- Two model families, deliberately small for this sample size: an interpretable
  linear baseline (regularized and quantile regression) and one gradient-boosting
  comparator, with a declared deterministic seed.
- Diagnostics: feature importance, probability calibration, quantile coverage, and a
  comparison against the trivial baseline.
- Versioned model artifacts carrying a content hash and full provenance — dataset
  version, feature configuration, split definition, hyperparameters, seed and code
  revision — so any artifact can be regenerated or rejected.
- A mandatory sample-size disclosure: observation count, effective sample size after
  accounting for label overlap, and per-pool counts.

## Acceptance

A deliberately injected future-derived feature is detected and fails the run; a label
window that overlaps the training fold without a purge and embargo gap is detected and
fails the run; features computed at time t are unchanged when all data after the label
horizon is truncated, so the harness is prefix-invariant; pool holdout reports
per-pool out-of-sample metrics, and the harness reports an honest failure when a model
does not beat the trivial baseline on an unseen pool; a saved configuration rerun
reproduces byte-equivalent artifacts after excluding declared observational fields; a
dataset that is not qualified for its declared numeraire is refused rather than
silently converted; and every report states its observation count, effective sample
size and per-pool breakdown.

Boundary and failure cases covered: a pool with a single episode; a horizon longer
than a pool's window; a feature window that predates the dataset start; a label whose
observability crosses the dataset end; an entirely missing feature column; a constant
feature; and a quantile level for which the sample is too small to estimate.

## Must not

Use a random, shuffled or otherwise non-temporal split; select hyperparameters on the
evaluation fold; tune, impute or normalize using statistics computed over the
evaluation period; present a model metric as strategy performance; allow a
floating-point value to enter a protocol, replay, accounting or valuation integer
path; hide a losing, degenerate or non-identifiable model; or report a point estimate
without the sample size and uncertainty that produced it.
